I enjoy building tools that remove clutter and make things nicer to use, on the web and in World of Warcraft.

---

### WoW: Forever addons
* **[Warlock Flyout](https://github.com/OD728/WarlockFlyout)**: One button for your warlock: every demon on left-click, Ritual of Summoning and your stones on right-click.
* **[Tidy Roll](https://github.com/OD728/TidyRoll)**: Clean, compact loot roll bars showing who picked what, and the winner.
* **[Skill-Up Colors](https://github.com/OD728/SkillUpColors)**: Colors recipe names orange, yellow or green by skill-up chance.
* **[Cooldown Skin](https://github.com/OD728/CooldownSkin)**: A cleaner look for the game's Cooldown Manager.
* **[XP Rail](https://github.com/OD728/XPRail)**: A movable XP / reputation bar with rested and quest XP.
* **[Haversack](https://github.com/OD728/Haversack)**: All your bags in one small window, sorted into a few categories.

### Web
* **[CleanTwitch](https://github.com/OD728/CleanTwitch)** and **[STwitchT](https://github.com/OD728/STwitchT)**: Less clutter and tidier stream titles on Twitch.
* **[YouTube Userscripts](https://github.com/OD728/youtube-userscripts)**: Default speed, auto-pause, 1440p and a 5-per-row grid.
* **[Firefox Compact](https://github.com/OD728/FirefoxCompact)**: Compact tabs and an address bar that hides until you need it.
* **Web Development**: Building and maintaining my personal domain **[itmeron.de](https://itmeron.de/)**.
* **Customization**: Experimenting with **Catppuccin** themes and user-centric styling.
